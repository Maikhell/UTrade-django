U-TRADE PLATFORM: SELLER TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE

(For Central Student Government & Recognized Student Societies)

CONFIDENTIAL: Internal campus operations only within CvSU Bacoor.

EFFECTIVE DATE: Academic Year 2026–2027

1. Acceptance of Seller Terms

Welcome to the merchant portal of U-Trade, the official e-commerce platform of Cavite State University – Bacoor Campus. By registering, accessing, or utilizing a Seller Dashboard as a representative of the Central Student Government (CSG) or a recognized student society, you agree to be bound by these Seller Terms and Conditions. These terms derive their authority directly from the platform bylaws and are concurred by the Office of the Vice President for Business and Resource Generation (OVBRG).

2. Seller Eligibility, Authentication, & Dashboard Access
  • 2.1 Institutional Authentication: All seller accounts must be registered and accessed using official CvSU institutional credentials to preserve a closed-loop, secure campus ecosystem.
  • 2.2 Organizational Tier Restrictions: Seller privileges are strictly limited to verified accounts belonging to the Central Student Government (CSG) and recognized Student Societies.
  • 2.3 Dashboard Security: Financial ledgers, business generation metrics, and organizational management tools are isolated exclusively to authorized seller dashboards. Unauthorized access attempts will result in immediate administrative intervention.
3. Mandatory Merchandise and Pricing Approvals
  • 3.1 Pre-Approval Requirement: The CSG and all recognized student societies acting as sellers on U-Trade must submit all proposed merchandise, items, and exact selling prices to the Business Generating Office for rigorous review and formal written approval prior to publishing any listings on the platform.
  • 3.2 Absolute Pricing Authority: The Business Generating Office maintains absolute authority over pricing controls to protect student consumer welfare and guarantee fair trade practices across the campus. Any unapproved listings or unauthorized price modifications will be removed immediately by platform administrators.
4. Transactions, Receipts, and the 3% System Hosting Fee
  • 4.1 Payment Processing: The platform supports cash-upon-meetup transactions and verified digital payment integrations. The system automatically generates and stores digital receipts for all completed sales.
  • 4.2 Mandatory 3% Platform Fee: To support continuous server maintenance, system security, and platform operations, a mandatory 3% Platform fee is automatically computed and retained per transaction.
  • 4.3 Monthly Settlement Deadline: All accumulated financial obligations, fees, and hosting deductions must be cleared directly with the Campus Cashiering Office on or before the 5th day of each succeeding month.
5. Fulfillment Protocols and Campus Safety Standards
  • 5.1 Designated Drop-Off Zones: To maintain campus security, pedestrian safety, and an undisturbed academic atmosphere, all physical exchanges, item hand-offs, and buyer-seller meet-ups for orders processed via U-Trade must occur strictly within official campus drop-off zones designated by the administration (e.g., Student Center Hub or Gazebo areas).
  • 5.2 Strict Delivery Restrictions: Unregulated classroom-to-classroom deliveries, building-to-building drops, and hallway peddling are strictly prohibited.
  • 5.3 Prohibited Commodities: Listing, marketing, or selling weapons, illegal drugs, alcoholic beverages, tobacco, vape products, or unverified perishable food items is strictly banned. Violations will trigger immediate account freezing.
6. Seller Violations and Disciplinary Sanctions

6.1 Grounds for Penalties: Failure by the CSG or any student society to secure prior Business Generating Office price approval, attempting to bypass platform transaction workflows, or defaulting on the monthly platform fee settlement deadline will invoke immediate administrative action.

6.2 Administrative Penalties: Violations of these seller terms shall result in:

  • Immediate freezing of the organizational U-Trade dashboard and suspension of merchant selling privileges.
  • Formal account suspension from the platform.
  • Direct referral to campus disciplinary authorities and the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) for institutional review.

U-TRADE PLATFORM: DATA PRIVACY NOTICE AND COMPLIANCE POLICY

In Compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012

CONFIDENTIAL: For internal campus operations only within CvSU Bacoor.

EFFECTIVE DATE: Academic Year 2026–2027

1. Introduction and Commitment to Privacy

Cavite State University – Bacoor Campus respects your right to privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of all students, faculty, staff, and organizational members who utilize the U-Trade e-commerce platform. This Data Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, process, and protect your personal information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

2. Personal Data Collected

To ensure a secure, closed-loop campus ecosystem, the U-Trade platform collects the following types of information:

  • 2.1 Account Registration Data: Official CvSU institutional credentials, student/employee ID numbers, university email addresses, and encrypted passwords.
  • 2.2 Profile and Verification Data: Full name, degree program/year level, department/office, and verification status managed by university databases.
  • 2.3 Transactional Data: Digital receipts, order histories, product/service listings, pricing details approved by the Business Generating Office, and automated system hosting fee logs.
  • 2.4 Behavioral and System Logs: Automated tracking metrics for order cancellations, unfulfilled meet-ups, chat histories between buyers and sellers, and login timestamps.
3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is collected and processed strictly for legitimate institutional and platform-related purposes, which include:

  • 3.1 Authentication and Security: Verifying user identities to maintain a closed campus ecosystem and preventing external fraudulent accounts.
  • 3.2 Role Management: Enforcing strict access privileges across system tiers (Campus Administration / Business and Resource Generation Office, CSG & Student Societies, and Standard Campus Users).
  • 3.3 Marketplace Operations: Facilitating peer-to-peer trading, digital receipts, product listing approvals, and communication between campus buyers and sellers.
  • 3.4 Accountability and Enforcement: Monitoring cancellation trends to apply progressive buyer penalties, tracking monthly system fee settlements, and executing campus safety protocols.
4. Data Sharing, Disclosure, and Access
  • 4.1 Internal Access Only: Personal data is strictly accessible to authorized platform administrators, the Campus Business Generating Office, internal auditors, and designated IT system operators.
  • 4.2 Disciplinary Referrals: In the event of consecutive buyer cancellations (third offense) or severe violations of platform rules by the CSG, student societies, or individual users, relevant data and incident reports will be officially transmitted to the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) or campus disciplinary authorities.
  • 4.3 Third-Party Restrictions: Personal data will never be sold, traded, or shared with external commercial entities, advertisers, or individuals outside the CvSU Bacoor academic ecosystem.
5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention
  • 5.1 Security Measures: The platform enforces technical, organizational, and physical security measures—including encrypted passwords, secure database hosting, and dashboard isolation—to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  • 5.2 Retention Period: Personal data and transaction logs shall be retained for the duration of the active academic year (Academic Year 2026–2027) and archived in accordance with university document retention policies and auditing requirements.
6. Rights of the Data Subject

As a user of the U-Trade platform under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you possess the following rights:

  • 6.1 Right to Be Informed: You have the right to be notified of how your personal data is gathered and processed.
  • 6.2 Right to Access: You may request access to your personal account profile, order logs, and system warning histories.
  • 6.3 Right to Correction: You have the right to update or correct inaccuracies in your institutional profile data through authorized campus registration channels.
  • 6.4 Right to Object / Erasure: You may object to the processing of your data or request account deactivation, subject to any pending administrative, financial (e.g., unpaid hosting fees), or disciplinary obligations.
7. Contact Information

For questions, concerns, or data privacy inquiries regarding the U-Trade platform, you may contact the Campus Business Generating Office or the designated Data Protection Officer through the Office of the Campus Administrator at CvSU Bacoor Campus.
