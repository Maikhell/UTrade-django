Notice: By registering for UTrade, you agree to comply with all university policies and the regulations set forth below.
1. Eligibility & Verification

Access to UTrade is strictly limited to verified members of the University community. Users must provide valid and proper identification. Providing false credentials or attempting to register with expired or future-dated identification will result in immediate account suspension.

2. Prohibited Listings

To maintain a safe academic environment, the following are strictly prohibited:

  • Alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, or related paraphernalia.
  • Academic materials including test banks, leaked exams, or commission-based academic services.
  • Counterfeit goods, weapons, or hazardous substances.
3. Transaction & Payment Policy

UTrade facilitates a manual payment verification system via online payment. Users are responsible for verifying payment receipts before completing hand-overs. UTrade does not act as an escrow and is not liable for financial discrepancies between buyers and sellers.

4. Liability & Safety

Users are encouraged to conduct meet-ups in high-traffic, well-lit areas on campus. UTrade provides the platform for connection but does not guarantee the quality, safety, or legality of the items advertised. All trades are conducted at the user's own risk.

5. Data Privacy

Your personal information, including Student Numbers and CVSU email addresses, is collected solely for verification and system communication purposes. We do not share your data with third-party entities outside of university-approved administrative oversight.
